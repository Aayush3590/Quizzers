01WHY WE MADE IT

A simple way to check what you know.

QUIZZERS is for answering multiple-choice questions about programming concepts. It turns familiar topics into short quizzes, so you can check your understanding without needing to write or run code.

Built as a student project, it keeps the experience straightforward: pick a topic, answer questions, get feedback, and see your score.
